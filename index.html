about me

Hi there! I’m Shivam, aka Tangy, and I’m from Mauritius 🇲🇺! (but I’ll keep exactly where a secret!).


I’m a tech lover who’s always tinkering with Android, Windows, Linux, and web stuff. I know some basic HTML and CSS, and I’m pretty handy with tools like WordPress.


I like making cool, functional websites—not for commissions, though. It’s just a hobby to learn, have fun, and sometimes kill time.


I’m also a gamer! I play Genshin Impact, Minecraft, Roblox, and Among Us. If you’re looking for a gaming buddy, I’m your person!


I also post on YouTube, TikTok, and socials, but only when I feel like it—so yeah, my socials are more “chill” than active. ;)
